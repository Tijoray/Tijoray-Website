Tijoray

SMS opt-in and message programme

Tijoray is an operating name of GEN Z DIAMONDS COMPANY LIMITED.

Tijoray sells NFC-enabled fine jewellery. Each piece is registered to the mobile number of the person it was made for, and the owner uses the Tijoray mobile app to open the memories, certificates and documents stored with that piece.

Where consent is collected

Consent is collected inside the Tijoray mobile app, at the moment the customer hands over their number. There is no other route by which a number enters the messaging programme.

  1. The customer signs in to the Tijoray app with their email address, Google, or Apple.
  2. The app asks for a mobile number, and the customer types in their own.
  3. The consent disclosure below sits directly beneath the button, on that same screen.
  4. The customer taps Send Code. That tap is the opt-in, and it is what causes the one-time passcode to be sent.
  5. The customer types the code back into the app, which confirms the number and links the pieces registered to it.

Numbers are never purchased, rented, or imported from third parties, and no number is added from any other source. A customer who does not enter a number and request a code is never messaged.

The consent disclosure

By tapping Send Code you agree to receive text messages from Tijoray at this number.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for any delays or undelivered messages.

What we send

Account verification. A one-time passcode, sent when the customer requests it, confirming that the number they entered is theirs. Example: Your Tijoray confirmation code is 123456. This code expires in 10 minutes.

Account notifications. Occasional service messages, to customers who have confirmed a number, about the delivery and servicing of the piece they own. Example: Tijoray: your piece has shipped and will arrive Tuesday. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Message frequency varies; typically one verification message per customer at sign-up. All messages are transactional and initiated by the customer's own action. No marketing or promotional messages are sent, and no mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Stopping messages, and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to be removed immediately; you will receive one confirmation and nothing after it. Reply HELP for help, write to support@tijoray.com, or call us on +1 (647) 825-2510.
